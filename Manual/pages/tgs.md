# Hilt: The Graflex Shop
***
## Navigation: [Manual](../README.md) / tgs.md
***

These are the TGS hilts we have tested with the Secura Chassis. There may be other hilts this works with but are untested. 
Some minor modifications will be needed in some cases - in all cases, thus far, the modifications are only to the chassis parts or parts, not to the hilt itself. 

If you are trying to figure out Folmer vs Non-Folmer or patent vs non-patent one of the best guides is from Wannawanga. 
They also happen to have some of the best/most accurate parts for Graflex builds, go check them out! 
Here are some of their links:
- [Wannawanga Lightsaber Guide](https://wannawanga.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/SW_Lightsaber_Guide.1.16.17.pdf)
- [Wannawanga Shop](https://wannawanga.com/shop/)

## Part Modifications
***
> [!IMPORTANT]
> - very minor modifications needed
> - replacement URL listed ONLY if you mess your up - you do not need to buy if you purchased one of the above items

| **Part Name**                 | **Replacement Part URL**                                         | **NOTES**                                                                                  |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Graflex Slide Switch Assembly | https://graflexshop.com/products/graflex-slide-switch-assembly   | Needs to be cut down to fit in the side-switch area and move freely.                       |
| Graflex Brass Sync Contacts   | https://graflexshop.com/products/copy-of-graflex-brass-sync-pins | minor trimming/filing may be needed based on your build.                                   |
| Graflex Red Button            | https://graflexshop.com/products/graflex-red-button              | you will need to extend the throw distance - a M3x4 works very well and is adjustable      |

> [!CAUTION]
> - Modifications may not be able to be reversed - proceed carefully and at your own risk.
