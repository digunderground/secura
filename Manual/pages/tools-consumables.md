## Tooling
The tooling you use with the above list of tools 
***
> [!IMPORTANT]
> - Parts marked as "required" will be needed
> - Parts marked as "optional" will make the job easier
> - Any items without links is due to either the item not being offered.

| **Tooling**             | **Required** | **URL Example**        | **NOTES**                                                                                                                                 |
|-------------------------|--------------|------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 4-40NC tap and drill    | YES          | https://a.co/d/fnsp2cP | for the blade retention screw + 4-40 rods. Drill and then tap, 3d SLM is not perfect.                                                     |
| #3-48 Tap and drill     | YES          | https://a.co/d/dVFXWlp | For the graflex brass pins                                                                                                                |
| M1.4 x 0.3mm            | YES          | https://a.co/d/728u6F4 | to hold on the speaker enclosure - VERY fragile, take your time                                                                           |
| Cobalt Drill Bits 1.4mm | Optional     | https://a.co/d/j2wcuOR | to secure the secondary micro switch - optional based on how you implement that.                                                          |
| 10Pcs Carbide Burr Set  | YES          | https://a.co/d/fVOcT8a | required, good for cleaning print mistakes, sharp edges etc.                                                                              |

> [!CAUTION]
> - Tapping metal is difficult and requires precise alignment - failure to do so will snap your tap and may (will likely) scrap your piece
> - Tapping Difficulty increases with metal selection - Stainless and Titanium are VERY difficult - you WILL snap taps.
