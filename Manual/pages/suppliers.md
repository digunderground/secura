## Laser Cut Parts
***
> [!NOTE]
> - You can print these parts, though we highly recommend getting laser cut parts 
> - The design utilizes 0.040" (1mm) thick metal - you can go thicker and omit fins for a different look
> - the Hex mesh is optional - but great for the glass eye and plasma gate feature 
> - the hex should always be aluminum, its easier to bend and work with, it is the thinnest material available
