## FDM/SLS materials 
***
> [!IMPORTANT]
> - Ideally FDM/SLS are only used for the ISO (isolation) parts
> - A full SLS/FDM chassis will work but will be significantly less rigid.
> - you will need to merge some parts for a full resin chassis.

| **MATERIAL**          | **METHOD**   | **APPROVAL** | **TESTED** | **NOTES**                                                          |
|-----------------------|--------------|--------------|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| PA12                  | SLS/FDM      | YES          | YES        | non-condustive, resistant to heat, does not deform over time       |
| PA6                   | SLS/FDM      | YES          | YES        | non-condustive, resistant to heat, does not deform over time       |
| ABS                   | FDM          | YES          | YES        | non-condustive, resistant to heat, does not deform over time       |
| ASA                   | FDM          | YES          | YES        | non-condustive, resistant to heat, does not deform over time       |
| PLA                   | FDM          | NO           | YES        | Non-Condustive, deforms under heat, deforms over time              |
| PETG                  | FDM          | NO           | YES        | Non-Conductive, Handles heat well, deforms over time, not ideal    |
| ANY Carbon Reinforced | SLS/FDM      | NO           | YES        | POTENTIALLY conductive - DO NOT USE                                |
| PC (polycarbonate)    | FDM          | YES          | YES        | non-condustive, resistant to heat, does not deform over time       |
| PEEK                  | FDM          | YES          | NO         | Of course this will work, you are borderline crazy                 |

> [!NOTE]
> This is NOT an exhaustive list, other materials may work.
> List will be updated over time

## Metal materials
***

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> - we have only tested SLM due to its strength and ability to be machined if needed. 
> - we have NOT tested DMLS / LMM / DMP / or Binder Jet - due to costs, availability or known limitations


| **MATERIAL**          | **METHOD** | **IMPACT** | **TESTED** | **NOTES**                                                     | **EXAMPLE COST** |
|-----------------------|------------|------------|------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|
| Stainless 316         | SLM        | Harder     | YES        | Durable, difficult to work with, moderate expense             | $180             |
| Aluminum              | SLM        | Easy       | YES        | Moderately Durable, easy to work with, low expense            | $160             |
| Brass                 | SLM        | Expensive  | NO         | Moderately durable, easy to work with, very high expense      | over $1000       |
| Copper                | SLM        | Expensive  | NO         | Moderately durable, difficult to work with, very high expense | over $1000       |
| Bronze                | SLM        | Expensive  | NO         | Durable, difficult to work with, very high expense            | over $1000       |
| Titanium              | SLM        | Expert     | YES        | Extremely durable, very difficult to work with, high expense  | $675             |

## Resin materials
***
> [!IMPORTANT]
> - Resin should NOT used for the ISO (isolation) parts due to their tendency to fracture
> - A full resin chassis will work but will be significantly less rigid and prone to cracking.
> - you will need to merge some parts for a full resin chassis.

| **MATERIAL**                         | **METHOD** | **APPROVAL** | **TESTED** | **NOTES**                                                                               |
|--------------------------------------|------------|--------------|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Nylon Like                           | SLA        | YES          | YES        | relatively durable, less prone to cracking                                              |
| ABS Like                             | SLA        | YES          | YES        | relatively durable, less prone to cracking                                              |
| Siraya Tech Tenacious Flex           | SLA        | NO           | YES        | difficult to print details, durable, flexible                                           |
| 50/50 ABS/Siraya Tech Tenacious Flex | SLA        | YES          | YES        | durable, relatively easy to print, best option for SLA                                  |
| RESIONE G217 Clear ABS-Like Resin    | SLA        | CLEAR ONLY   | YES        | Great for clear parts after sanding and polishing, not recommended for the full chassis |


> [!NOTE]
> This is NOT an exhaustive list, other materials may work.
> List will be updated over time
